## HyperTile 0.4.2

- Prices show in your currency (€, £, ₹, C$, A$, ¥), matching checkout

## HyperTile 0.4.1

- HyperTile now speaks Spanish, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese, following your Mac's language
- Fixed: if the trackpad stopped reporting in the middle of a swipe (sleep, unplugging it, pausing HyperTile), the sliding desktop picture could stay over the screen. It now springs back and goes away
- Default desktop names ("Desktop 2") are translated too

## HyperTile 0.4.0

- Try HyperTile free for 2 days: every feature works from the first launch, with no licence key
- Swiping between desktops now follows your fingers and slides, like macOS Spaces; keyboard switches slide too
- The desktop map shows each desktop as it really looks, with its name
- Name desktops (⌃⌥⌘D, double-click a name); named desktops stay when their windows close
- In the map, drag a window onto another to swap them, or onto another desktop to move it
- ⌃⌥⌘W, or a stack's badge, shows windows hidden behind others so you can bring one to the front
- Dragging a window highlights the tile it will land in, and the window there slides into your old tile so you can see the swap
- Smoother swipe: follows your fingers frame by frame and keeps their speed when you let go
- Windows go back to their desktops after HyperTile restarts, or after a pause
- The window picker can move a window to another desktop, float, fill the screen, minimize or close it
- Optional Screen Recording permission for real window pictures in the map and swipe

## HyperTile 0.3.0

The first public release.

- Tiles every window automatically, in Grid, Columns, Rows or Side layouts
- Optional virtual desktops, with a four-finger swipe between them
- Keyboard shortcuts to swap, focus, float and resize windows
- Windows with a minimum or fixed size get room in the layout
- Picks up trackpads connected after launch, and after sleep
- Uses less energy when nothing on screen changes
- Offers to move itself to Applications
- Licence activation in the welcome tour and Settings → Licence
